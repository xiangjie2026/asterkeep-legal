Adding a day

Open the app, type a sentence the way you would say it — for example “My mom’s birthday is May 18” — and tap Find the date. Check the confirmation card, then tap Save. You can also add a date manually at any time.

添加一个日期

打开 App，像平时说话一样输入一句话（例如“我妈妈 5 月 18 日生日”），点 Find the date，核对确认卡后点 保存。你也可以随时手动新增。

Editing or deleting

Tap any memory to open it. Use Edit to change the date, person, repeat rule or reminder. Use Delete to remove it; the app asks for confirmation first, because a deleted reminder cannot be restored by us — we never had a copy.

修改或删除

点开任意一条记忆即可编辑日期、人物、重复规则与提醒。删除前 App 会二次确认——被删除的提醒我们无法帮你恢复，因为我们从未持有副本。

A reminder did not arrive

Check, in this order:

  1. iOS Settings → Notifications → Asterkeep → Allow Notifications is on, and the alert style is not Delivered Quietly.
  2. A Focus mode (Do Not Disturb, Sleep, Work) is not silencing the app.
  3. Low Power Mode is off, or the reminder is not scheduled during it.
  4. The device date, time and time zone are correct.
  5. The event has not been deleted, archived, or had its reminder removed.

Asterkeep reminders are local notifications. They depend on iOS and on the device being powered on. For anything critical, please also keep a second reminder — the app is a helper, not a guarantee.

提醒没有响

请按顺序检查：

  1. iOS 设置 → 通知 → Asterkeep → 允许通知已开启，且未设置为“安静送达”。
  2. 是否开启了专注模式（勿扰、睡眠、工作）屏蔽了本 App。
  3. 是否处于低电量模式。
  4. 设备日期、时间与时区是否正确。
  5. 该事件是否已被删除、归档，或提醒被移除。

Asterkeep 的提醒是本地通知，依赖 iOS 与设备开机状态。重要事项请另外保留一道提醒——它是助手，不是保证。

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. There is no account and no cloud copy held by us.

数据存在哪里

只在你的 iPhone 上。没有账号，我们也不持有云端副本。

Export and import

Settings → Your data → Export my data (JSON) produces a file you can keep or move to another device. Import uses the same format. Keep the file safe: it contains the names and dates you entered.

导出与导入

设置 → 你的数据 → 导出我的数据（JSON），可得到一份可保存或迁移的文件；导入使用同一格式。文件包含你录入的姓名与日期，请妥善保管。

Deleting everything

Settings → Your data → Delete all local data. This cannot be undone.

删除全部数据

设置 → 你的数据 → 删除全部本地数据，操作不可撤销。

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings → Asterkeep Pro → Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to the app.

恢复购买

设置 → Asterkeep Pro → 恢复购买。购买绑定的是你的 Apple 账户，不是 App 本身。

Chinese lunar dates

Asterkeep converts lunar dates using iOS’s built-in Chinese calendar. Leap months are handled explicitly: when a year has no matching leap month, the app uses the rule you chose. If a converted date looks wrong, please send us the lunar date and the year — this is the one area where regional calendars can differ.

农历日期

Asterkeep 使用 iOS 内置的中国历法进行转换；闰月按你选择的规则显式处理，某年没有对应闰月时不会静默猜测。若转换结果异常，请把农历日期与年份发给我们——这是地区历法差异最容易出问题的地方。

Languages

English is the primary language. Simplified Chinese is available in Settings → Language. Dates follow your device’s region settings.

语言

以英文为主，简体中文可在 设置 → 语言 中切换。日期格式跟随设备的地区设置。
