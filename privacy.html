Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-14 · Last updated: 2026-11-14

Timoori is a personal date keeper. It is built so that the dates you care about never leave your device.

The short version

  • No account. No sign-up. No login.
  • The people, dates and notes you add are stored only on your iPhone.
  • We do not collect, transmit or sell your personal data.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS, not by a server.
  • The only payment involved is a one-time App Store purchase handled by Apple.

1. Who we are

Timoori is published by an independent developer (“we”). For any privacy question, contact styr12@163.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

1. 我们是谁

Timoori 由独立开发者（下称“我们”）发布。隐私相关问题请联系 styr12@163.com，我们会在两个工作日内回复。

2. Information you create, stored on your device

When you add a memory, the following may be stored in the app’s local database on your device:

  • a person’s name and, if you choose, relationship and notes;
  • event titles, dates, calendar type (Gregorian or Chinese lunar), repeat rules and reminder times;
  • text you type into the input field.

This information is stored locally. It is not uploaded to us. We have no server that receives it and no way to read it.

2. 你创建的信息，保存在你的设备上

当你添加一条记忆时，以下信息可能存入 App 在你设备上的本地数据库：

  • 人物姓名，以及你自愿填写的称呼关系与备注；
  • 事件名称、日期、历法类型（公历或农历）、重复规则与提醒时间；
  • 你在输入框中键入的文字。

以上信息均本地保存，不会上传给我们。我们没有接收这些信息的服务器，也无法读取。

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise or coarse location, advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, usage analytics, crash reports, or the content of your memories. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or attribution SDK. If any SDK is added later, this policy and the App Store privacy label must be updated before release.

3. 我们不收集的信息

我们不收集：姓名、邮箱、手机号、通讯录、精确或粗略位置、广告标识符、设备指纹、使用统计、崩溃日志，以及你记忆内容本身。App 内不含任何第三方广告、统计或归因 SDK。若未来引入，必须在发版前同步修改本政策与 App Store 隐私标签。

4. Microphone and speech

Voice input is optional and only starts after you tap the microphone and grant permission. Speech is transcribed on device using Apple’s speech framework; audio is not stored by us and not sent to us.

4. 麦克风与语音

语音输入为可选功能，只有在你点击麦克风并授予权限后才会启动。语音通过 Apple 的语音框架在设备端转写；我们不保存音频、也不上传音频。

5. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your device. Their content is generated on device. Apple’s notification service may relay the delivery of a notification, but does not receive your memory database. You can revoke notification permission at any time in iOS Settings; the app remains usable without it.

5. 通知

提醒是由 iOS 在你设备上排程的本地通知，内容在设备端生成。Apple 的通知服务可能参与投递，但不会获得你的记忆数据库。你可以随时在 iOS 设置中关闭通知权限，关闭后 App 仍可正常使用。

6. Purchases

Timoori Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple. We receive only the information Apple provides to every developer (for example, that a purchase occurred and the transaction identifier). We never see your card number, billing address or Apple Account password. Refunds are handled by Apple under the App Store terms.

6. 购买

Timoori Pro 是通过 Apple 处理的一次性非消耗型购买。我们只能获得 Apple 向所有开发者提供的信息（例如发生了购买、交易标识符），无法看到你的卡号、账单地址或 Apple 账户密码。退款由 Apple 按 App Store 条款处理。

7. Backups

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, your app data may be included in that backup. Backups are controlled by you and Apple; we cannot access them. Disabling backup or deleting the backup removes that copy.

7. 备份

若你使用 iCloud 或电脑备份设备，App 数据可能被包含在备份中。备份由你与 Apple 控制，我们无法访问。关闭备份或删除备份即可移除该副本。

8. Your choices

  • Edit any memory inside the app at any time.
  • Delete a single memory, a person, or all local data from Settings → Your data.
  • Export your data as JSON from Settings, if you want a portable copy.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its local database from the device.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, and no data request is needed.

8. 你的选择

  • 修改：随时在 App 内编辑任意一条记忆。
  • 删除：在设置 → 你的数据中，删除单条记忆、单个人物，或全部本地数据。
  • 导出：在设置中导出 JSON，获得一份可携带的副本。
  • 卸载：卸载 App 即从设备移除其本地数据库。

由于我们从不接收你的数据，服务端没有可删除的内容，也无需提交数据请求。

9. Children

Timoori is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

9. 儿童

Timoori 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

10. International users

Your data stays on your device, wherever your device is. We do not transfer personal data across borders because we do not receive it. The App Store purchase is processed by Apple under its own terms.

10. 跨境与地区

你的数据保存在你的设备上，无论设备在哪个国家或地区。我们不会跨境传输个人数据，因为我们不接收这些数据。App Store 购买由 Apple 按其条款处理。

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this URL. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.

11. 政策变更

若本政策发生变化，我们会更新上方日期并在本 URL 发布新版本。重大变更会在 App 更新说明中注明。
